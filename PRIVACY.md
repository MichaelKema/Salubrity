# Privacy

Salubrity has no account, analytics, advertising, or cloud diary synchronization. Your diary stays on your computer unless you copy or share it yourself.

The installed Mac app stores its diary in `~/Library/Application Support/Salubrity/nutrition.json`. The data is not encrypted by Salubrity. macOS account protections and disk encryption remain important if you share or lose your computer. Back up the diary file while the app is closed; deleting this file removes your saved foods, meals, entries and goals.

Optional online food search sends the search text to Open Food Facts through the bundled backend over HTTPS. Open Food Facts receives normal network information, including your IP address. Imported product records stay in your local diary. Salubrity does not send your diary, goals, or logged meals to the search service. Food search results are external label data; verify them against the package label.

Fonts and illustrations are bundled; the app does not fetch fonts from external servers. This release has no automatic update mechanism.
